Why ChatGPT answers you

In 2022, OpenAI taught GPT-3 to follow instructions with the help of about 40 people. Here is the whole trick, in pictures.

InstructGPTOpenAI · Mar 2022ChatGPT · Nov 2022

Ouyang et al., OpenAI, 2022 · arXiv:2203.02155 · “Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback”

Both replies are OpenAI’s own example (Jan 27, 2022). The same question runs through the paper’s Figure 2.

Why it gave homework

GPT-3 read the internet and learned one game: guess the next word. So your question looked like line one of a worksheet, and it wrote more lines.

The paper’s word: misaligned

The fix: 3 steps

This is the paper’s Figure 2, made simple. About 40 people did the teaching.

1

Copy

People write great answers. The model copies them.

~13,000 answersSFT: supervised fine-tuning
2

Rank

The model writes 4 to 9 answers. A person sorts them, best to worst. A second AI learns to guess the winner: the judge.

33,000 promptsreward model (6B)
3

Practice

The model writes, the judge scores it, the model adjusts. Over and over, on a leash.

31,000 promptsPPO: reinforcement learning

Sorting is a shortcut

Every pair in a ranking is one free “this beats that”. Slide to see how many.

36 matchupsK choose 2 = 9 × 8 ÷ 2

Formula 1: the judge

loss(θ)=−1(K2)E(x,yw,yl)∼D [log(σ(rθ(x,yw)−rθ(x,yl)))]

Looks scary. It just says: score the winner above the loser. yw is the answer people picked, yl the one they didn’t, and σ turns the gap into the chance a person agrees.

92%chance a person agrees with the judge

Gap values are illustrative; the formula is Eq. 1 of the paper.

Formula 2: the leash

objective(φ)=E(x,y)∼DπφRL[rθ(x,y) −βlog(πφRL(y|x)/πSFT(y|x))] +γEx∼Dpretrain[log(πφRL(x))]
r(x, y)The judge’s score. Get more of it.
− β · log(…)The leash. Chase a score too hard and you learn to game it, so you lose points for drifting far from step 1.
+ γ · E[…]Keep reading the internet, so old skills stay. Getting polite cost some skill; the paper calls that the alignment tax. This term (PPO-ptx) pays most of it back.

Did it work?

85%

of the time, people picked InstructGPT’s answer over GPT-3’s (both 175B).

100× smaller

The 1.3B InstructGPT still beat the 175B GPT-3.

41% → 21%

Made things up half as often on tasks with the facts in front of it.

< 2%

of GPT-3’s training compute: 65 vs 3,640 petaflop/s-days.

Not perfect: ask why you should eat socks after meditating, and it will happily explain.

Nine months later

On Nov 30, 2022, OpenAI launched ChatGPT, trained “using the same methods as InstructGPT”. That’s why it answers you.

Bigger didn’t fix AI. People did.
