InstructGPT on one page

How roughly 40 people ranking answers taught GPT-3 to help

Ouyang et al., OpenAI, 2022 · arXiv:2203.02155 · “Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback”

The problem

GPT-3 is trained to predict the next word, so it continues your prompt instead of doing what you asked. The paper calls this misaligned, and a bigger model doesn’t fix it.

1SFT

Copy good examplessupervised fine-tuning

Labelers write ideal answers to prompts (about 13k of them), and GPT-3 is fine-tuned to imitate them.

2RM

Learn a judgethe reward model

The model writes K = 4 to 9 answers to a prompt and a labeler ranks them, best to worst (33k prompts). Every pair is a lesson: K choose 2, up to 36 per ranking. A 6B model learns to score answers.

The paper, equation 1
loss(θ)=−1(K2)E[logσ(rθ(x,yw)−rθ(x,yl))]
loss(θ)=−1(K2)E[logσ(rθ(x,yw) −rθ(x,yl))]

In words: score the winner (yw) above the loser (yl). σ turns the score gap into the chance a person would agree.

3PPO

Practice against the judgereinforcement learning

Reinforcement learning (PPO) on 31k prompts: write an answer, get a score from the reward model, adjust. The objective adds two guard rails:

The paper, equation 2
objective(φ)=E[rθ(x,y)−βlog(πφRL(y|x)πSFT(y|x))] +γE[logπφRL(xpretrain)]
objective(φ)=E[rθ(x,y) −βlog(πφRL(y|x)πSFT(y|x))] +γE[logπφRL(xpretrain)]
  • βThe leash (a KL penalty): stay close to the step-1 model, so it can’t game the judge. The paper calls that gaming over-optimization.
  • γKeep practicing on the original internet text. That’s PPO-ptx, the final InstructGPT: it wins back most of the “alignment tax” on old skills (SQuAD, DROP, HellaSwag, WMT French→English).

What it got

85%

of the time, people preferred InstructGPT 175B over GPT-3 175B (give or take 3%)

1.3B > 175B

the small InstructGPT beat GPT-3, a model over 100× bigger

21% vs 41%

how often it made things up vs GPT-3, on closed-domain tasks

< 2%

extra compute: 4.9 + 60 vs GPT-3’s 3,640 petaflop/s-days

Not perfect: it still plays along with false premises. Ask “Why is it important to eat socks after meditating?” and it explains.

What came next: nine months later ChatGPT launched, trained “using the same methods as InstructGPT” (OpenAI).
